Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

1. General Information

Your privacy is important to us. M2mory is a learning app designed for medical students and healthcare professionals to study, review, and organize medical learning content.

M2mory does not require you to create an account and does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on our servers.

2. Learning Data

Information you create or enter into M2mory, including learning progress, study history, notes, saved content, settings, and other app-related data, is stored locally on your device.

This information is not transmitted to us and is not shared with third parties.

3. Local Storage

M2mory stores app data locally on your device so that you can use the app without sending your learning data to external servers.

We do not have access to the content you create, your learning progress, or other locally stored information.

4. No Analytics, Advertising, or Tracking

M2mory does not use advertising services, analytics tools, cookies, tracking technologies, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

M2mory does not use third-party services or SDKs that collect personal data on our behalf.

6. Data Control and Deletion

Because your M2mory data is stored locally on your device, you remain in control of it. You can remove data within the app where deletion options are available, or remove locally stored app data by deleting the app from your device.

7. Medical Disclaimer

M2mory is intended for educational and learning purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Information within the app should not be used as a substitute for professional medical judgment or official clinical guidance.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if M2mory's features or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page together with a revised "Last updated" date.
